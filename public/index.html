How Ghost Works

Ghost is a bandwidth sharing platform that allows you to earn money by sharing your unused internet bandwidth.

How it works:

  • When you Start Service, the Ghost application runs a secure data-sharing engine in the background
  • This engine shares a small portion of your unused bandwidth with our secure network
  • You earn points based on the amount of data shared and connection time
  • Points are converted to real money that you can withdraw via PayPal, bank transfer, or cryptocurrency

Key Features:

  • Safe & Secure: Your personal data is never shared or accessed
  • Background Operation: Runs quietly without interrupting your internet usage
  • Bandwidth Control: Set limits to control how much bandwidth is shared
  • Real-time Earnings: Watch your balance grow in real-time

Minimum Requirements:

  • Stable internet connection
  • Minimum $10 balance for withdrawals
  • Service must be active to earn points
