How Ghost Works

Ghost is a bandwidth sharing platform that allows you to earn money by sharing your unused internet bandwidth.

How it works:

  • When you Start Service, the Ghost application runs a secure data-sharing engine in the background
  • This engine shares a small portion of your unused bandwidth with our secure network
  • You earn points based on the amount of data shared and connection time
  • Points are converted to real money that you can withdraw via PayPal, bank transfer, or cryptocurrency

Key Features:

  • Safe & Secure: Your personal data is never shared or accessed
  • Background Operation: Runs quietly without interrupting your internet usage
  • Bandwidth Control: Set limits to control how much bandwidth is shared
  • Real-time Earnings: Watch your balance grow in real-time

Minimum Requirements:

  • Stable internet connection
  • Minimum $10 balance for withdrawals
  • Service must be active to earn points

About Ghost Technology Bright SDK

Ghost Platform Technology

Ghost utilizes the Bright Data SDK to enable secure and efficient bandwidth sharing. This enterprise-grade technology ensures:

  • Secure Data Transmission: All shared bandwidth is encrypted and anonymized
  • Resource Optimization: Only unused bandwidth is utilized, never affecting your personal internet experience
  • Transparent Operation: Clear metrics showing exactly how much bandwidth is being shared
  • Enterprise Security: Military-grade encryption protects all data transmissions

Bright SDK Integration

The Bright Data SDK is only activated when you explicitly press "Start Service" and runs exclusively during active sessions. It includes:

  • Automatic self-checks for system compatibility
  • Real-time bandwidth monitoring and limiting
  • Secure connection establishment with Ghost servers
  • Automatic cleanup when service is stopped

Privacy & Transparency

Ghost is committed to complete transparency:

  • We never access your personal files or browsing data
  • All earnings are calculated based on verifiable metrics
  • You can stop the service at any time with one click
  • Full control over how much bandwidth you wish to share

Compliance & Safety

Ghost operates in full compliance with data protection regulations including GDPR and CCPA. Our technology has been reviewed and approved for safe consumer use.
